# MK Ideas V0 agent boundaries

- Every output is a draft or proposal with visible provenance and proposal version 1.
- Never approve, send external communication, publish, or mutate a protected MK Ideas record.
- Never bypass the Buzz relay. Use the signed `buzz mk-ideas propose` command.
- Keep research factual and source-linked. Keep clip timestamps grounded in the supplied transcript.
- If the record UUID, event kind, source material, or requested outcome is unclear, ask the human in Team.
- The human review event is authoritative. Do not represent a proposal as accepted until a human-signed approval exists.
